# Content and abuse policy

FPO Parakh helps assess farmer producer organisations across governance, business operations, processes and financial performance. Its content is private to the organisation running the deployment: nothing users submit is published by the software.

- **Who moderates.** Each organisation running a deployment owns and moderates its content. Its administrators can review submissions and delete FPO records.
- **Reporting.** Anyone can report inappropriate or illegal content to the operator of the deployment. Problems with the software itself, or with this project's own spaces, go to dpg@invarya.com.
- **Illegal content.** Operators remove it and report it where the law of their country requires.
