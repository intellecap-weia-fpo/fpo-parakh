# Content and abuse policy

FPO Parakh helps assess farmer producer organisations. What users submit is private to the organisation running the deployment, and the software does not publish it, apart from FPO profile details and scores on public listings.

## What counts as inappropriate or illegal

Content that is illegal under the law of the deployment's country. Also content unrelated to the assessment, abusive content, other people's personal data without a lawful basis, and forged documents.

## How it is found and handled

- **Finding it:** administrators review submissions and uploaded documents before an FPO is verified. Anyone can also report content.
- **Reporting:** report content to the organisation running the deployment. Problems with the software or this project's spaces go to dpg@invarya.com.
- **Removing it:** administrators reject the submission or delete the FPO record.
- **Illegal content:** operators remove it and report it where the law requires.
